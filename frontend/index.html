How Qubit works — first time here?
  1. Pair this device once: open Pair device, paste the webui.token from ~/.config/qubit-voicebridge/voice.yaml, and choose Save & test. Your token is stored only in this browser.
  2. Talk or type below. Hold the button and speak, or type a task and press Enter.
  3. Watch progress in the transcript and on Activity. Read-only work can run automatically; anything sensitive always asks first.
  4. Review reports and Reports. Use Settings only when you need to change models, voices, permissions, or pairing.

Everything runs on your PC. No account is required, nothing is sent unless you act, and every change is backed up and reversible.
