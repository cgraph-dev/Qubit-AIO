# Competitor Fee & Protection Benchmark — captured 2026-08-08

Verified from official help-center pages on 2026-08-08 via real browser.
Re-verify before relying on them — schedules change (PlayerAuctions updated
fees Aug 19, 2026). Compare TOTAL cost (seller + buyer + withdrawal).

## Seller fees

### ZeusX (help.zeusx.com — "What are the seller fees?")
- Top-Ups: **5%** flat.
- Accounts / Items / Coins / Services: **5–10% by seller tier**:
  Iron 10% (default) → Bronze 9% (>$300 sales) → Silver 8% (>$1K) →
  Gold 7% (>$10K) → Platinum 6% (>$100K) → Vibranium 5% (invite-only).
- Withdrawal: **1.5% + $3** (bank transfer / e-wallet / Payoneer / PayPal-Tipalti).

### Eldorado (support.eldorado.gg — "Seller fees")
- Currency: **5%**; Top-up & Gift Cards: **0%**; Items/Accounts/Boosting: **10%**.
- Game-specific: reduced (OSRS/WoW gold 1.5–3%), increased (Roblox family
  15%, boosting LoL/CoD/OSRS 20%, GTA5 accounts 30%).
- Fees only on completed orders.
- **Buyer payment fee: 8% + $0.30 flat** (card/Apple Pay/crypto); balance
  portions avoid it. This is why 0% seller fee ≠ cheap — total ~8%+ on buyer.

### PlayerAuctions (support.playerauctions.com — "Seller Fees")
- Currency/Item **9.99% + $0.99**; Accounts/Boosting **12.99% + $0.99**;
  **Top Up 4.99% + $0.99**. Fee update effective Aug 19, 2026.
- Disbursement ~4% + $0.50/order (some 3%); wire $25 fee, min $100; other
  min $35–50.

### G2G (support.g2g.com — payout schedule article)
- Seller-credit model: accounts hold **14 days**, other products
  **15 min–24 h**; auto-complete **3 days** after delivery if buyer silent.
- Commission % is dashboard-only; disbursement/tax region-dependent.

### G2A (blocks automation; public data only)
- Seller fee dashboard-only (~10.9% widely reported — UNVERIFIED).
- Observed marketplace prices: Apple Gift $5 → $5.63 (+12.6%), $10 → $11.41
  (+14.1%); Win11 Pro $23.11 (list −85%).
- **G2A Plus membership: $2.99/mo, $15.99/6-mo, $28.99/yr** — up to 10% off
  keys, free monthly key/DLC, discount codes, priority support.

## Protection / trust systems

| System | Mechanics |
|---|---|
| Eldorado **TradeShield** | free, automatic; covers fraud/chargebacks; fund-release holds: Gift Cards 4d, Accounts 5d, GTA5 14d, Fortnite/R6S 10d, CS2 items 10d, Roblox Limiteds 14d, Roblox currency 7d |
| PlayerAuctions **7-step escrow** | buyer pays PA → payment verification → seller delivers (accounts inspected by PA) → buyer confirms → PA disburses |
| G2G **seller credit + auto-complete** | credit released 15min–24h (14d accounts); auto-complete 3d post-delivery |
| ZeusX **Premium Seller** | badge + premium-only filter + L2 support; requires 90-day account, >$10K AND 100 sales, feedback ≥4.9, <2% disputes; reversible |
| G2A **Plus** | PAID membership (contrast: earned-rank model is the differentiator) |

## Derived positioning example (RankValut)

- Gift cards/top-ups: base **4.5%** (below ZeusX 5%+wd, PA 4.99%+$0.99+4%;
  far below Eldorado 0%+8% buyer total).
- Items/currency: **7.5%**; Accounts: **10%**; Services: **10%**.
- Zero buyer fee at launch; fee floor ~3.4% (payment ~2.9% + 0.5pp ops);
  rank discounts 0–7pp floor-capped per category (low base fees cap gift-card
  discount at ~1.1pp — big rank savings live in accounts/services).
