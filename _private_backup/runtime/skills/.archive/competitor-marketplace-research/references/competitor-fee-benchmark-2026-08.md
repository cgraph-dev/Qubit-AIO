# Competitor fee & protection benchmark (verified 2026-08-08)

Captured from official competitor help-center pages on 2026-08-08 via a real
browser. Fee schedules change — re-verify before relying on them (e.g.
PlayerAuctions announced a fee update effective 2026-08-19). When comparing,
use TOTAL transaction cost (seller fee + buyer fee + withdrawal), not seller
fee alone — some marketplaces hide margin on the buyer side.

## ZeusX (help.zeusx.com — "What are the seller fees?")

- Top-Ups (direct + E-Pin): **5%** flat.
- Accounts / In-game Items / Game Coins / Gaming Services: **5–10% by seller tier**.
- Seller tiers: Iron 10% (default) → Bronze 9% (>$300) → Silver 8% (>$1K) →
  Gold 7% (>$10K) → Platinum 6% (>$100K) → Vibranium 5% (invite/admin only).
- Withdrawal: **1.5% + $3** (bank transfer / e-wallet GCash,MAYA,DANA,OVO,GoPAY /
  Payoneer / PayPal via Tipalti).
- Premium Seller (help.zeusx.com "What is a ZeusX Premium Seller?"): badge on
  store/profile, buyers can filter Premium-Seller-only listings, dedicated L2
  officer (soon). Requirements: account ≥90 days, >$10K sales AND 100 sales,
  feedback ≥4.9, <2% post-sales disputes. Reversible.

## Eldorado (support.eldorado.gg — "Seller fees")

- Currency **5%**; Top-up & Gift Cards **0%**; Items / Accounts / Boosting **10%**.
- Game-specific: some reduced (OSRS/WoW gold 1.5–3%), many increased
  (Roblox-family 15%, boosting LoL/CoD/OSRS 20%, GTA5 accounts 30%).
- Fees only on completed orders.
- Buyer payment fee: **8% + $0.30** flat on card/Apple Pay/crypto; balance
  funded portions avoid the fee. (This is why "0% seller fee on gift cards"
  is misleading — total is ~8% on the buyer side.)

### Eldorado TradeShield (support.eldorado.gg "TradeShield (Selling)")

- Automatic, free, no manual activation. Covers fraudulent payments,
  unauthorized transactions, chargebacks, payment-provider disputes; platform
  handles provider communication for the seller.
- Category fund-release periods: Gift Cards 4d; Accounts (default) 5d;
  GTA5 Accounts 14d; Fortnite/R6S Accounts 10d; CS2 Custom Items 10d;
  Roblox Limiteds 14d; Roblox Currency 7d.

## PlayerAuctions (support.playerauctions.com — "Seller Fees" + "Disbursements")

- Commission on completed orders: Currency 9.99%+$0.99; Item 9.99%+$0.99;
  Accounts 12.99%+$0.99; Boosting 12.99%+$0.99; **Top Up 4.99%+$0.99**.
- Fee-structure update announced effective **Aug 19, 2026** (watch this page).
- Disbursement: ~4%+$0.50 per order (some methods 3%); wire $25 initial bank
  charge min $100; other methods min $35–$50.
- 7-step escrow: buyer pays PA → payment verification → seller delivers
  (accounts submitted to PA for inspection first) → buyer confirms → PA
  disburses.

## G2G (support.g2g.com — "What Is G2G's Payout Schedule...")

- Payment issued as **Seller Credit** when order reaches Completed.
- Release: Accounts **14 days**; other products **15 min – 24 h**.
- Auto-complete: order auto-marked complete **3 days** after full delivery if
  buyer silent.
- Disbursement fee + tax region-dependent; gateway/intermediary bank fees
  extra; crypto network fee paid by seller; PayPal final. Sellers must be
  verified to withdraw.
- Seller commission % is dashboard-visible, not published.

## G2A (g2a.com — blocks automated access)

- Seller fee percentages are dashboard-only (widely reported ~10.9% + payment
  fees; NOT independently verified 2026-08-08). Mark as unverified if cited.
- Observed public prices 2026-08-08: Apple Gift Card $5 → $5.63 (+12.6% over
  face), $10 → $11.41 (+14.1%); Win11 Pro $23.11 (−85% from $150.29 list).
- G2A Plus membership (archived official page): **$2.99/mo, $15.99/6-mo,
  $28.99/yr**; benefits: up to 10% off keys, free key/DLC monthly, exclusive
  discount codes, priority support. (Paid membership, unlike earned-rank
  models.)

## Positioning pattern used for RankValut

- Base seller fee = verified competitor TOTAL − 1–2 percentage points per
  category: gift cards/top-ups 4.5%, items/currency 7.5%, accounts 10%,
  services 10%; zero buyer fee at launch; fee floor = payment rate + 0.5pp
  (~3.4%); rank-ladder discounts 0–7pp floor-capped; withdrawal fee zero at
  launch (competitors charge 1.5%+$3 / 4%+$0.50).
- Protection system parity: Valut Protect holds 4d (gift cards) / 7d (items) /
  14d (accounts); auto-complete 3d; dispute 48h + 24h extension.
