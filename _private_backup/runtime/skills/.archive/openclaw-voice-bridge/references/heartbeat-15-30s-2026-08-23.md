- `_HUM_PHRASES` 10 -> 22, `_BOSS_PHRASES` 10 -> 12 (boss ~"Got it boss, moving~").

## Pitfall
Don't confuse TTS slowness (1.04s OpenAI network) with heartbeat spam. Heartbeat only fires while `task.active` (big task). Casual `talk` is not throttled by heartbeat intervals; its wait is `tts.synthesize` only.
