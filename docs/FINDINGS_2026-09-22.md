### The 94 `GATED` routes are policy refusals, not gaps

Sampling shows they are **deliberate Qubit policy** (security + single
authority), each with a working counterpart: `/api/pty`+`/api/console` →
`PTY_NO_SHELL` (Qubit Chat / `qubit tui`); `/api/ws` → `WS_SINGLE_PATH` (SSE);
`/api/config/raw`, `/api/env/reveal`, `/api/credentials/pool.*` →
`SECRET_NEVER_SERVED` (`qubit auth status`); `/api/providers/oauth.*` →
`OAUTH_MANUAL_HANDOFF`; `/api/skills/hub/.*`, `/api/mcp/catalog/install` →
`BLOCKED_REMOTE_SOURCE`; `/api/hermes/update.*` → `UPDATE_OPERATOR_OWNED`
(the update surface is now the canonical `/api/qubit/update*` routes — see G6).

**Plan correction:** "close the 94 GATED routes" is wrong. Each must be
**dispositioned**: (a) a legitimate refusal the owner confirms, with a working
counterpart and a voice path, or (b) a genuine gap that must be implemented.
Implementing the refusals literally (raw PTY, secret reveal, remote fetch) would
violate Qubit's own policy.
